# BA ĐỘNG — Coastal Escape visual direction

## World

Coastal editorial: a tactile travel journal shaped by salt air, warm paper, deep water, and the quiet confidence of a field guide. The interface should feel collected rather than templated.

## Palette

- Ocean 950 `#082D36` for footer and deepest contrast.
- Ocean 900 `#123C47` for headings, hero, and primary actions.
- Ocean 700 `#1D6971` for links and active controls.
- Seafoam 100 `#E3F0ED` for calm panels.
- Sand 50 `#FAF7F1` as the dominant paper-like surface.
- Sand 200 `#E9D9BD` for quiet dividers.
- Sunset 500 `#C88A51` as a sparing navigational accent.

## Type

Be Vietnam Pro handles Vietnamese body copy, controls, and display headings. The SRS permits a single family when a serif cannot be verified; the final browser audit found unreliable Vietnamese diacritics in the initial serif candidate, so the shipped system uses weight, scale, and spacing—not a second font—to create editorial hierarchy. Body copy stays readable and close to 65–75ch.

## Composition

Large photographic moments, asymmetrical editorial splits, clear white space, 12-column thinking on wide screens, and one-column reflow on narrow screens. Cards are used as content objects, not as the whole page grammar.

## Motion

One authored hero reveal, small transform-based hover feedback, and a short mobile menu entrance. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes non-essential transitions.

## Interaction language

Text-led links, compact pill filters, visible focus rings, keyboard-operable accordion/lightbox, and plain-language empty/error states. No fake ratings, booking actions, or decorative UI that implies unavailable services.
