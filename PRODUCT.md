# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: .NET 10 Blazor WebAssembly Standalone, C# and Razor components, static JSON, and GitHub Pages because the brief explicitly requires a backend-free static deployment.

## Users

Travel-curious visitors planning a short coastal trip; mobile readers looking for quick visual context; and lecturers evaluating a complete .NET frontend project.

## Product Purpose

BA ĐỘNG — Coastal Escape is an editorial-style digital guide to Ba Động and the surrounding Trà Vinh coastal context. Success means a first-time visitor understands the destination quickly, can browse places and food, and can shape a respectful trip without a booking backend.

## Positioning

The site uses a calm coastal editorial structure rather than a tour-selling template: a visual story, source-aware static content, and small planning tools that stay honest about what is not verified in real time.

## Operating Context

The site is assessed locally and on GitHub Pages at a repository subpath. Content is maintained by editing JSON and image references in the repository; visitors do not submit personal data.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Routes cover home, about, explore and destination details, cuisine and food details, gallery/lightbox, guide/FAQ, itineraries, contact/location, and a friendly 404. Search is case- and Vietnamese-diacritic-insensitive. There is no authentication, server API, database, booking, payment, or fake contact form.

## Brand Commitments

Name: BA ĐỘNG — Coastal Escape. Tagline: “Nơi biển chạm vào nhịp sống miền Tây.” Voice is warm, concise, specific, and transparent about illustrative assets and changing information.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied SRS is the product authority. Repository content includes a static JSON content model and a source ledger. The current build uses clearly labeled Unsplash illustrative images; exact Ba Động photography should replace them before public academic submission.

## Product Principles

1. Make the destination legible in seconds.
2. Let real content carry the emotion.
3. Treat local life and nature with respect.
4. Never invent prices, ratings, hours, or services.
5. Keep the architecture static, inspectable, and deployable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA in scope: keyboard-visible focus, semantic landmarks, skip link, labelled controls, alt text, 44px-ish touch targets, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts that remain usable at 320px and 200% zoom.
